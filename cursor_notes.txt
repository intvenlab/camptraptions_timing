## Design decisions
- MaxSequenceCount (was SequenceCount, maxSequences); FrameCount (was burstFrameCount); StartFrameSpacingMin (was interFrameDelay); PostShutterHalfPressHoldTimeExtension (was postBurstHalfPressHoldTime, AKA Z)

- No minStrobeRecycleTime; no maxWakeHalfPressHoldTime / no 30s activity cap
- minHalfPressBeforeShutter applies before every shutter activation
